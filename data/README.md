# Dataset

This folder holds the time-based flow feature files of the **ISCX VPN-nonVPN (ISCXVPN2016)** dataset, for the 15, 30, 60 and 120 second timeouts. The project results use `TimeBasedFeatures-Dataset-15s-VPN.csv` (18,758 rows and 24 columns; 9,793 VPN and 8,965 Non-VPN).

The dataset was created by the Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity at the University of New Brunswick and is not our work. Official source: https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/vpn.html

If you use the dataset, cite: G. Draper-Gil, A. H. Lashkari, M. S. I. Mamun, and A. A. Ghorbani, "Characterization of Encrypted and VPN Traffic Using Time-Related Features," in *Proc. ICISSP*, 2016, pp. 407-414.
